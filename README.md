## Selected products

### [MCQ Exam Builder](https://github.com/christosdvm/mcq-exam-builder)

A privacy-conscious exam-generation tool for educators who need to import, validate, review, shuffle, and export multiple MCQ exam versions from inconsistent question-bank formats.

**Problem:** Academic staff often prepare questions in Word, Excel, or plain text using inconsistent structures. That creates avoidable risk: formatting errors, incorrect option parsing, manual answer-key work, and time pressure close to exam delivery.

**Product direction:** A professional assessment utility that helps educators move from unstructured question banks to structured, validated, export-ready exams without sending exam content to external AI services.

**Engineering themes:** multi-format parsing, validation rules, reproducible randomisation, answer-key generation, DOCX/XLSX handling, educator UX, privacy-by-design.

**Status:** public portfolio repository with a live deployment and automated CI.

---

### [Voicemail SMS Auto Reply](https://github.com/christosdvm/Voicemail-SMS-Auto-Reply)

A workflow automation system for small professional clinics that detects voicemail notifications and sends controlled SMS follow-up during defined operating hours.

**Problem:** Clinicians often miss calls while consulting, teaching, travelling, or handling focused work. The result is a poor client experience and repeated manual follow-up, even when the required response is predictable.

**Product direction:** A practical communication automation layer that improves responsiveness while preserving professional boundaries, business-hour logic, duplicate protection, and privacy constraints.

**Engineering themes:** Gmail automation, SMS API integration, time-window logic, duplicate prevention, operational safety, privacy-conscious workflow design.

**Status:** public repository; evolving from internal workflow automation into a reusable clinic-communication pattern.

---

### Veterinary Logbook & e-Portfolio Systems

A structured competency-tracking framework for veterinary education, designed to support student-level evidence, progress dashboards, and programme-level analysis across successive years of training.

**Problem:** Competency-based education depends on reliable evidence of practical progression, but many logbooks remain fragmented, manual, difficult to analyse, or disconnected from curriculum quality assurance.

**Product direction:** A scalable academic logbook model that can grow from simple student dashboards into longitudinal programme analytics for skills, placements, cases, and clinical exposure.

**Product themes:** competency tracking, structured data, student dashboards, curriculum analytics, quality assurance, longitudinal education records.

## Product principles

```text
Start with the workflow, not the technology.
Build tools around real users and real constraints.
Automate repetitive work without hiding consequential decisions.
Make privacy, data handling, and failure modes explicit.
Prefer boring reliability over impressive fragility.
Ship, observe, learn, improve.
```

I am interested in software that becomes useful infrastructure for expert work — not just prototypes that look good in a controlled demo.
